    How to play

    A mystery country is picked each day. Guess any country and compare it to the answer.

    MatchCloseNo match
    • Population and Area: ▲ means the answer is bigger, ▼ smaller. Yellow is within 25%.
    • Coast: whether the country is landlocked.
    • Direction: arrow from your guess's capital toward the answer's capital. Distance is in km; yellow is under 2,000 km.
    • Guesses are unlimited. The daily puzzle changes at midnight local time.

    Figures are rounded approximations.
