    How to play

    A mystery Dancing with the Stars (US) celebrity is picked each day. Guess any contestant and see how they compare in 7 categories.

    MatchClose / partlyNo match
    • Season, Age and Finish: ▲ means the answer is higher, ▼ lower. Yellow is within 2 seasons, 5 years or 2 places.
    • Known as: what they were famous for. Yellow means they share at least one.
    • Age is their age when their season premiered.
    • Finish: 1 is the mirrorball trophy. Higher numbers went home earlier.
    • Guesses are unlimited. Daily puzzles change at midnight local time.
