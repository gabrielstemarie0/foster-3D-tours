How it works

  1. Book a time

    Tell us the address and which units need tours. We find a time that works around your tenants and showings.

  2. We scan the space

    We bring the camera and move through each room. You don't need to stage anything. Tidy up, turn the lights on, and we handle the rest.

  3. You get your tour link

    We process the scan and send back a link and an embed code, ready to post wherever you list.
