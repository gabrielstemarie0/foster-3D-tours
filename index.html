    Questions people ask

    Something else? Send us a message.

    How long does a scan take?

    About 1–2 hours for an average 2,000 sq ft home. Larger homes and commercial spaces take longer, and we'll give you an estimate up front.

    How should I prepare the property?

    Tidy up, turn on every light, open interior doors and clear walkways. Anything visible will be in the tour.

    When will I get my tour?

    Most tours are delivered within 24–48 hours of the scan. Rush processing is available for an extra fee.

    How do I share it?

    You get a link you can send by email or text, post on social media, or embed on your website and listing pages.

    How long does the tour stay online?

    One-time scans include a year of hosting, and extended hosting is available. Monthly plans stay live as long as you're subscribed.

    Where do you work?

    Across the New York metro area and Idaho's Wood River Valley. If you're nearby but not sure, ask and we'll let you know.
